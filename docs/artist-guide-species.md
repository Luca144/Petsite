# Creatures — a guide for the artist

This is for Skerro. It walks through building a whole species in the panel,
start to finish, with no code and nobody else involved. (This walkthrough is
also the site's promise for this milestone: if any step here doesn't work
the way it says, that's a bug — tell Katharina.)

---

## Start it

Panel → **Creatures** → type a name into **Start a new species** → *Start it
(as a draft)*. You land on its page. A **draft** is yours alone: players
cannot see it, buy it, start with it, or find it while exploring — however
long it stays a draft. Take your time.

## Build it, in any order, saving as you go

Everything about the species lives on this one page. Each box has its own
save button, so nothing is lost if you stop halfway.

- **Names & words.** The name, the flavour text (the sentence on its page
  and in the shop), and the lore — the long text for the library, which
  opens in a later milestone; write it now and it's waiting there.
- **One card per stage** (baby, juvenile, adult). Each takes a picture and,
  if you like, its own words — shown instead of the flavour text while the
  creature is at that stage. **Choose a picture** opens your art store,
  showing everything filed as *Creature sprite*. If the picture you want
  isn't there, upload it under **Pictures** first (export rules are in the
  pictures guide — loop forever, native pixel size, real transparency).
- **Library picture.** Same, from the *Library art* shelf.
- **Numbers & flags.** The gem price, whether players can get one, whether
  new players may start with one, and its favourite and least favourite
  treat. The treat lists are every feedable item; a dislike never hurts a
  creature — it eats, pulls a face, and loses nothing.

**Changing a picture later:** choose a different one on the stage's card.
The swap shows up everywhere at once — every page, every card, instantly,
for everyone. That's one click, by design.

## Look at it before anyone else does

**Preview on the real page** shows your species on the actual creature
page — not a mock-up, the real thing — with buttons to flip between baby,
juvenile and adult. Open the same address on your phone to check the small
screen. Do this before publishing; it's the whole point of the button.

## Publish it

The **Publish** button checks the species is ready: every stage needs a
picture, and it needs its name and flavour text. If something's missing,
the message lists exactly what — no guessing. Once published (and marked
"players can get one"), it's on the shop shelf within the minute.

**Changed your mind?** *Back to draft* takes it off the shelf again.
Creatures players already have keep living and looking exactly as they do —
nobody loses anything; new ones just can't be got until you publish again.

## If you make a mistake

Every save is kept in the **Versions** list at the bottom of the page
(the newest twenty). *Restore this* puts the species back to how it was at
that save — words, numbers and picture choices alike. Restoring never
publishes or unpublishes by itself.

## What you can't do (yet, or on purpose)

- **Alternate forms** (colour variants and the like) come with the library
  milestone — decided together, so a "form" means something.
- **Deleting a species** isn't offered. Players may own creatures of it;
  *Back to draft* is the way to retire one from circulation.
- A picture that a species is using can't be deleted from the Pictures
  screen — unlink it on the species page first. The message will say so.
