# M2.7 — Moderation tools (build plan)

*2026-08-31. Proceeding under the standing arrangement for the rest of M2
(rough review between increments, full test round after M2.9). This is
the most security-sensitive increment since M2.1; every decision below is
deliberately conservative.*

## What this builds

The Moderator role finally gets its tools (build plan M2.7):

1. **The report queue** (`/admin/reports`) — open reports ordered by
   priority then age, each showing WHAT was reported (the actual text or
   name, visible to staff even while hidden from players), how many
   DIFFERENT people reported it, and the reporter's own track record
   (X of their Y past reports were dismissed — the requirement recorded
   in ReportService's plan: spite-reporting must be visible, or the
   hide-on-report trade goes bad).
2. **Account history on one page** (`/admin/accounts/{id}`) — the
   pattern view: username, about text, every creature with its name and
   bio, guestbook entries they signed, reports about them (all statuses),
   reports they filed (with dismissal count), their notices, and every
   moderation action ever taken on them (read from the append-only audit
   log — no second history table to keep consistent).
3. **Actions**, each one POST, each audited, each with a plain-words
   confirmation:
   - **dismiss** a report (unhides what the report hid — a dismissed
     report must not leave someone's words invisible);
   - **remove text** (clear a creature bio / profile about, unhide, and
     send the notice saying so) — the plan's "warn" needs something the
     warning can point AT;
   - **force-rename** a player or a creature to a neutral default;
   - **warn** (a notice without any other change);
   - **mute** (timed: the player cannot write new free text — bios,
     about, names, guestbook signing — but plays on normally);
   - **suspend** (timed: the account cannot log in, and any live session
     ends at the next request);
   - **unmute / unsuspend** early.
4. **Official notices to players** — the one-way channel. A notice is a
   card at the top of every page (above the flash) until acknowledged;
   it says what happened in kind fixed words and points to the Felkyo
   email for appeals. **Notice text is chosen from a fixed set of
   templates keyed to the action, never free-typed** — the site's rule
   that no free-text channel exists between people is kept even for
   staff, the wording stays kind and consistent, and a hurried moderator
   cannot send a hurtful sentence. Players cannot reply; the card's only
   button is "I've read this".

## The three security questions (CLAUDE.md §7)

1. **Who is allowed, and how is it enforced?** All queue/history/action
   routes: `$adminGate->protect(Role::Moderator, ...)` — same chokepoint,
   authorised per request, all in the `AdminGateTest` matrix. The notice
   acknowledge route is the one PLAYER-facing addition: it requires the
   session user to OWN the notice (checked in SQL: `WHERE id = :id AND
   user_id = :me`), with CSRF and a rate limit.
2. **Worst a malicious actor could do?**
   - *A player* cannot reach any of it (gate matrix), cannot spoof a
     notice (notices render only from the notices table, which only the
     moderation service writes; the acknowledge route can only mark
     their own as read, never create), and cannot use acknowledge to
     probe other people's notice ids (own-row UPDATE, same silent
     outcome either way).
   - *A hostile moderator* is the new surface: every action is audited
     append-only with before/after; suspension/mute are timed (no
     permanent-without-review state); force-rename writes a neutral
     name, never chosen words; notices are templates, never typed. The
     owner reads the audit log.
   - *Replay/race*: actions are idempotent updates (muting twice = one
     state); dismiss+unhide uses one UPDATE each; no currency or items
     move anywhere.
   - *The mute/suspend columns* are read fresh per request (suspension
     in the front controller before dispatch, mute inside the text
     services), so a demoted/expired state acts immediately.
3. **What do the tests prove?** The gate matrix refuses non-moderators
   on every new route; a muted player's bio/about/rename/guestbook
   writes are REFUSED (each one tested) while petting still works; a
   suspended player cannot log in and a live session is ended on the
   next request; dismiss unhides; force-rename produces the neutral
   name and updates the impersonation skeleton; a player cannot
   acknowledge another's notice; notices appear until acknowledged and
   never after; every action writes its audit row.

## Schema (one migration)

- `users` gains `muted_until datetime NULL`, `suspended_until datetime
  NULL` (timed states; NULL = in good standing).
- `moderation_notices`: id, user_id FK, kind (the template key),
  created_at, read_at NULL. Indexed (user_id, read_at) — the per-page
  "any unread?" query.
- Reports table already has everything the queue needs (status,
  priority, created_at, about_user_id, unique-per-reporter).

## Honest limits, said out loud

- Username history starts NOW (the audit row keeps the old name);
  pre-M2.7 renames were never recorded anywhere and cannot be shown.
- Mute is enforced in the text-accepting services, not in some central
  middleware — the list of such services is short (bio, about, rename,
  guestbook) and each gets its own refusing test; a NEW free-text
  feature must add the check, and the developer guide says so.
- "Suspend" hides no content; it stops the account. Removing what they
  wrote stays a separate, deliberate act per thing.
