# Shops — a guide for the artist

This is for Skerro. It walks through setting up a whole shop in the panel,
in one sitting: its look, its keeper, and what is on the shelves when.

---

## The shop's page in the panel

Panel → **Shops** → pick a shop (or start a new one — it arrives as a
draft, invisible until published; today only the general store has a door
players can reach, more get theirs in a later milestone).

Everything lives on one page, each box with its own save button:

- **Names & words.** The shop's name and the line of description under it.
- **The keeper.** Their name, their **greeting** — spoken under the shop's
  name on the real page — and their **refusal line**, which is what the
  search says when it finds nothing ("Ruinily doesn't have anything like
  that on the shelves"). Write them in the keeper's voice; they are the
  shop's personality.
- **Pictures.** The keeper's portrait (upload it under **Pictures** as an
  *NPC portrait*) and the shopfront background (as an *Area background*).
  The background sits behind the shop's header like a painted scene; the
  words always sit on solid parchment on top of it, so nothing you paint
  can make them hard to read.
- **The shelves.** What is for sale. Put an item on with **Put it on the
  shelf** — only *published* items can be shelved (the page says so if one
  isn't) — and give it a **season** if you want: from-date, until-date, or
  leave both empty for all year round. Out of season an item simply isn't
  offered; players are never teased with "come back in March". Taking an
  item off the shelf never takes it away from anyone who bought one.

The general store's **See the real shop page** button is the preview: what
you saved is what players see, within the minute.

## Mistakes and history

Every save is kept in **Versions** (the newest twenty). *Restore this*
brings back that moment — words, keeper, pictures, **and the shelves as
they were**. Restoring never publishes or unpublishes by itself.

## Prices live on items, not shops

What something costs is set on the item itself (Panel → **Items**), because
an item has one price wherever it is sold. The shelf only decides *whether*
and *when* this shop offers it.
